# Privacy Policy for Nudge

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Nudge works without an in-app account. If you are signed in to iCloud, Nudge can use Apple's CloudKit service to keep your data in sync across your devices.

## Data Nudge handles

Nudge stores the reminders, Spaces, tags, preferences, location marks, and audio files you choose to import inside the app's local container. When iCloud is available, Nudge also stores a copy in your private CloudKit database so it can appear on your other devices. Private CloudKit data is controlled by your Apple Account, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is not visible to the developer.

## Data collection and tracking

Nudge does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal data to the developer. Nudge does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. Data sent to private CloudKit is processed by Apple to provide iCloud sync and is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## Apple system services

Nudge uses Apple's notification, AlarmKit, Core Location, document-picker, and CloudKit services. Location marks are used by the operating system to deliver arrival or departure reminders; Nudge does not send your location to the developer.

## Deleting your data

You can delete reminders and imported sounds inside Nudge. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from that device, but synced data may remain in your private iCloud database and on your other devices until it is deleted through Nudge or your Apple Account's iCloud data controls.

## Changes

If Nudge later adds an online service or changes how it handles data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure must be updated before that version is released.
