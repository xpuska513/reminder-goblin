# Privacy Policy for Nudge

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Nudge is designed to work without an account or external server.

## Data Nudge handles

Nudge stores the reminders, lists, tags, preferences, and audio files you choose to import inside the app's local container on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's reminder and custom-sound features.

## Data collection and tracking

Nudge does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal data to the developer. Nudge does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

## Apple system services

Nudge uses Apple's notification and document-picker services. Your device settings and any device backup service you enable are controlled by Apple and governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

You can delete reminders and imported sounds inside Nudge. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to any backups managed by your device or Apple account.

## Changes

If Nudge later adds an online service or changes how it handles data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure must be updated before that version is released.
